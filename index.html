Private Product / Football Intelligence SPECIAL PROJECT

CalcioSapien

A private football intelligence and decision-governance platform designed to help sporting departments make evidence-led recruitment decisions.

The problem

Football recruitment involves fragmented data, scouting observations, financial considerations and multiple decision-makers. CalcioSapien explores how those inputs can become a structured, traceable decision process.

Product and architecture

Developed using React and Python/FastAPI, with persistent data workflows, football evidence ingestion, player research, scouting observations, recruitment shortlists, scenario comparison and decision reporting.

Governance and engineering

The application models approval authority, role-based permissions, security boundaries and audit history. The engineering work also includes backend tests, CI validation, containerisation and documented backup and recovery procedures.

Responsible AI

Analysis distinguishes inspectable, rule-based scoring from optional model-assisted intelligence. Decisions remain subject to evidence quality and human review.
